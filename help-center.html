Frequently Asked Questions

Go to the Marketplace page and click the + Sell / List button in the top right. Fill in the item details, set a price in FCFA, upload a photo, and click Publish. Your listing will be visible to all students immediately.

Open the product details page and click the 💬 Message Seller button. You'll need to be logged in. A conversation will be created with the seller, and you can chat directly in the Messages page.

All prices on CampusConnect are displayed in FCFA (Franc CFA). When listing an item or service, enter the price in FCFA. Prices are formatted with commas for readability (e.g., 45,000 FCFA).

Go to the Referrals page and copy your unique referral link. Share it with friends. When they sign up and complete a transaction, you both earn referral credits which can be used within the platform.

On any listing or user profile, click the three-dot menu (⋮) and select Report. Choose a reason from the list and submit. Our moderation team reviews reports within 24 hours.

Go to the Forgot Password page, enter your university email address, and check your inbox for a reset link. The link expires after 1 hour for security.
