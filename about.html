Our Mission

Why We Built CampusConnect

University life is expensive, competitive, and fast-paced. Students constantly need to buy affordable textbooks, sell items they no longer need, find part-time jobs, and connect with peers who have skills they need.

We built CampusConnect to solve all of this in one place — a trusted, students-only platform where everything happens within your campus ecosystem.

All transactions use FCFA — the currency your campus runs on.
