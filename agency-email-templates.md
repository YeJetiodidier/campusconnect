# CampusConnect Agency Email Templates

Here are professional email templates that your team can use to reach out to agencies, companies, and HR departments to invite them to post jobs and internships on CampusConnect.

---

## Template 1: Initial Outreach (Inviting a Company to Post)

**Subject:** Connect with Top Student Talent at CampusConnect

Hi [Name/HR Team],

I hope this email finds you well.

I’m reaching out from **CampusConnect**, the central digital platform where students actively search for internships, entry-level jobs, and campus opportunities.

We noticed [Company/Agency Name]’s commitment to fostering young talent, and we would love to feature your upcoming job and internship openings directly on our platform. By posting on CampusConnect, your listings will instantly reach a highly engaged, motivated pool of university students who are eager to kickstart their careers.

Would you be interested in a quick 5-minute chat next week to see how easily you can list your opportunities with us?

Best regards,

**[Your Name]**  
[Your Title], CampusConnect  
[Your Contact Information]  
[Website Link]

---

## Template 2: Welcome/Onboarding Email (Once they agree)

**Subject:** Welcome to CampusConnect! Here’s how to post your listings.

Hi [Name],

Welcome to CampusConnect! We are thrilled to partner with [Company/Agency Name] to help connect our students with your incredible career opportunities.

To get started, you can easily post your jobs and internships directly to our network:

1. Log in to your recruiter portal at: [Link to Platform]
2. Click on **"+ Post a Job or Internship"** on your dashboard.
3. Fill in the required details and hit publish! Your listing will be instantly visible to our students.

If you have any questions or need assistance maximizing the visibility of your listings, please don't hesitate to reply directly to this email.

We look forward to a successful partnership!

Warmly,

**[Your Name]**  
[Your Title], CampusConnect

---
