1. Overview

📌 Short version: CampusConnect collects only the information needed to run the platform. We never sell your personal data. All data is stored securely and you can request deletion at any time.

CampusConnect ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users ("you", "student", "user"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are.

By registering on CampusConnect, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Name, university email address, year of study, faculty, and profile photo.
  • Listings & transactions: Items you post for sale, services you offer, and prices in FCFA.
  • Messages: Content of messages exchanged between users on the platform.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, device type, and browser information.
  • Referral data: Who referred you and who you referred.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the CampusConnect platform.
  • Enable messaging between students.
  • Display your listings and profile to other students.
  • Send you important notifications about your account or listings.
  • Improve platform features based on aggregate usage patterns.
  • Enforce our Community Guidelines and prevent fraud.

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information only in these cases:

  • Other students: Your name, profile photo, and listing details are visible to verified students on the platform.
  • Firebase (Google): Our backend infrastructure provider. Data is stored under Google's privacy and security standards.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect user safety.

5. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Google Firebase servers with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We implement:

  • Firebase Authentication for secure login.
  • Firestore Security Rules to ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Regular security reviews of our platform.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your personal data is removed within 30 days.

6. Cookies

CampusConnect uses minimal cookies to:

  • Maintain your login session (via Firebase Authentication tokens).
  • Remember your theme preference (dark/light mode).

We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Deletion: Request that your account and all associated data be deleted.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@campusconnect.app.

8. Minors

CampusConnect is intended for university students aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete their account immediately.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify users via the Announcements page. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: privacy@campusconnect.app
  • Help Center: campusconnect.app/help-center
